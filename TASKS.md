# Dotadex tasks

Goals for the site (`dota2-model-viewer`) and the dataset export (`dota2-model-tracker`).

Status: `[ ]` open · `[~]` in progress · `[x]` done · `[-]` cancelled (with the reason).
Every task has an ID. Done and cancelled tasks move to the archive at the bottom; nothing is deleted.

Order: cosmetics first (every particle and effect as in the game), abilities as in a match later.

---

## Particles: data (export, `dota2-model-tracker/particles.js`)

- [ ] **P-04 Control point values from items (`particle_control_point`).** 34 items set a CP, often a color (Doom's Molten Fracture: CP26 = `1.0 0.9 0.7`). Into the catalog, set on the site.
- [ ] **P-05 Particle materials (`.vmat`).** 236 effects (7%): models with an override material (`m_hOverrideMaterial`, 130), ground projections (`RenderProjected`, 158), `RenderCables`, `RenderBlobs`. Export the material's parameters and textures. Until then a model with an override material whose own material is a stand-in (`gray_25`, white, `dev/...`) is hidden: Dawnbreaker's Red Dawn weapon drew its fire cone as a white shell.
- [ ] **P-06 Model hitboxes.** Used by `CreateOnModel` (329), `LockToBone` (237), `MoveToHitbox` (41), `SetHitboxToModel`/`Closest`; we use random vertices instead. Read them from the model data next to the attachments (`readAttachments`).
- [ ] **P-07 `attribute_controlled_attached_particles`.** 828 entries in `items_game`: which attachments an effect's CPs follow. Needed for unusual courier/ward effects.
- [ ] **P-08 Status-effect color warps (`colorwarp_*`, 6 textures).** Volume textures (z slices) for `RenderStatusEffect`; do them with the status effects (A-02).
- [ ] **P-09 "What's missing" report in the export.** Count the resources effects refer to (textures, models, snapshots, materials) that the dataset lacks, next to `coverage.json`, so gaps show up by themselves.
- [~] **P-10 Animations of particle models** (`m_ActivityName`, 58; `m_sequenceName`). With V-07: `m_ActivityName` on `RenderModels` picks the sequence with that activity from `.sequences.json` (21 renderers). Left: `m_sequenceName` and activities on other operators. `m_bScaleAnimationRate` taken as frames for each 1000 units the particle moves (inferred from the files: 250 to 2000 on birds, fish and butterflies, the default 30 on projectiles); butterflies and Pangolier's parrot flapped 10 to 20 times too fast.

## Models and other

- [ ] **M-04 Animated materials (`DynamicParams`).** A `.vmat` can drive its parameters by expressions of time the game runs every frame; the export keeps only the fixed ones, so they stand still on the site. Seen on the Prismatic Kirin set's rainbow rim (`mh_antimage_rainbow_rim.vmat`: `g_vTexCoordOffset = frac(float2(.0325,0)*time())`, the detail layers scrolling `-.055` and `.055` the other ways, `g_flMaterialCloakFactor` by `sin`) and likely Alchemist's flasks. Needs: the export to write `DynamicParams` (and `DynamicTextureParams`) into the model's and skins' material data; the site to evaluate the expressions (`time()`, `sin`, `frac`, `lerp`, `float2`…) each frame into the material's uniforms (texture and detail offsets first). Measure first how many materials have them and which parameters they drive.
- [ ] **M-03 Terrorblade's default blade glow on other weapons (open question).** 8 of his 9 weapons create the default weapon's effects in `items_game` (`terrorblade_ambient_sword_blade`, `_blade_2`: a model of glowing planes made for the default blades), so the site draws that glow round blades of other shapes (seen with a set's crescent blades). Nothing in the data hides or reshapes it. Left as the data says until someone sees in the game that it is not there.
- [ ] **M-02 Hero forms and summons on the hero page.** Everything should be seeable: Lycan's wolf, Dragon Knight's dragon, Terrorblade's demon, Lone Druid's true form, Undying's golem, Phoenix's egg, Death Prophet's ghost, Visage's familiar, Witch Doctor's and Juggernaut's wards (`ability*`, `shapeshift`, `summon` slots), with their cosmetics. They are no longer put on the hero's skeleton (commit `2200723`). Agreed with the user on 2026-10-17: a "Form" switch on the hero page when the hero has such slots (Hero / each form by its slot's name); a form is shown as its own model (the chosen cosmetic or the default) with its effects, and choosing a cosmetic of that slot in Change Outfit switches to it.
- [ ] **M-01 Head showing through the helmet.** Seen on Anti-Mage with a helmet; common across the site. After the particles.
