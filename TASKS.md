# Dotadex tasks

Goals for the site (`dota2-model-viewer`) and the dataset export (`dota2-model-tracker`).

Status: `[ ]` open · `[~]` in progress · `[x]` done · `[-]` cancelled (with the reason).
Every task has an ID. Done and cancelled tasks move to the archive at the bottom; nothing is deleted.

Order: cosmetics first (every particle and effect as in the game), abilities as in a match later.

- [x] **P-11 Sheet frames squeezed to the first one's size.** `buildTexture` laid a sheet's frames in a grid of cells the first frame's size; frames differ within a sheet, so Ember Spirit's fire (`fire_particle_10_low`, a first frame of 15x22, flames up to 103x110) came out 5 to 7 times smaller and blurry; 145 of the 174 sheets have frames of different sizes.
